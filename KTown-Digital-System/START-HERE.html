Your 5-minute presentation

Open the demo wall full screen (press F11 in Chrome or Edge). Everything you click is the real system.

  1. Show the table card and the menu.

    Hold up the printed QR card for Table 2, then point to the phone on the left: the welcome, the lake photo, the drinks with their own photos, three languages at the top.

  2. Order like a guest.

    Tap + on Mango juice and Ice latte, open the order and press Place order. Reception chimes and the order appears with the table number.

  3. Reception sends it to the kitchen.

    Press Send to kitchen. The kitchen screen chimes. Press Start, then Ready.

  4. The guest is told where to pay.

    The phone pops up “Your order is ready” with the MoMo number 0783 094 301. Tap Where to pay, then I’ve paid, and leave 5 glasses of feedback.

  5. The owner sees everything, from anywhere.

    Reception taps MoMo received. The owner panel shows the money received and the feedback. Press Sample week (top bar) to show the weekly report, then change a price in Menu and watch the phone update.

Good to show as well: Reserve a table, Call staff (reception gets an alert), Events and offers (a new headline appears on the menu), Tables and QR (rename a table), the printed menu for guests without a smartphone.

What is in the system

For guests

  • Digital menu in English, French and Kinyarwanda, with K.Town’s own photos
  • Order from the table: each table has its own QR code
  • Live order tracking, then a “ready, here is where to pay” pop-up
  • Pay with MTN MoMo in one tap (number and amount filled in), or at the counter
  • Feedback that goes straight to the owner, plus a Google review link
  • Reserve a table, call staff, ask for the bill, see events and offers
  • Works on slow connections and keeps the menu visible offline

For K.Town

  • Reception screen: confirm orders, serve, confirm payments, bookings, alerts
  • Kitchen screen: big tickets with timers, start and ready, sold-out switch
  • Owner dashboard on phone or computer: money received today, last 7 days, weekly report with CSV and print
  • Edit the menu: prices, photos, sold out, new sections, translations
  • Tables: 4 to start, add, rename, remove; QR codes made automatically
  • Events, promotions and new dishes shown at the top of the menu
  • Guest contacts (with permission) and ready-made WhatsApp/SMS messages for events

For guests without a smartphone

  • A classic printed menu made from the same live menu, always up to date
  • Reception enters their order (New order), so every sale is counted
  • Paper feedback cards; staff type them in under Feedback

Brand

  • Brand guidelines: logo use, colours from the logo, fonts, tone of voice, photography, QR rules
  • Logo files for print and screens in assets/img/brand
  • QR table cards, poster, printed menu and feedback cards in print-kit

Before you present

  • Open this folder in Chrome or Edge (double-click START-HERE.html).
  • Open the demo wall and press Start the demo, so the chimes can play.
  • Press Sample week once, so the owner’s reports have a week to show. Remove it afterwards in the owner dashboard.
  • Test the MoMo button once on your own Android phone: it should open the dialer with *182*1*1*0783094301*amount#. If MTN uses a different shortcut, change it in Settings, Payment.
  • Print one table card on thick paper (print-kit/ktown-table-cards.pdf, actual size) to hold up.

The QR codes in the print-kit open https://ktown-karongi.netlify.app. Put the folder online under that name (Go live, step 7, takes 2 minutes) and they work on any phone, even before Firebase: a guest alone on a phone sees the demo order move to “ready” by itself. If you pick another address, print new cards from Owner dashboard → Tables & QR.

Confirm with your uncle

  • The name shown on the MoMo account (now “K.Town”).
  • The phone and WhatsApp number (taken from Google Maps: 0791 207 272).
  • Opening hours (from Google Maps: 09:00–23:30 every day).
  • The Wi-Fi name and password to show guests (optional).
  • The drinks descriptions, and have a native speaker read the French and Kinyarwanda once.
  • Which wines, beers and food to add: Menu, Add a menu section.

Demo mode and going live

Right now the system runs in demo mode: everything works and every screen opened in this browser stays in sync, which is perfect for presenting on one computer. To connect guests’ phones, the kitchen tablet, reception and the owner’s phone anywhere, switch on live mode with a free Firebase project and put the folder online. It takes about 30 minutes.
