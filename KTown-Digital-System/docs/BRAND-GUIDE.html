The brand

Where Lake Kivu meets the glass

K.Town is a liquor boutique, café and terrace above Lake Kivu in Karongi. Guests come for a glass of wine at sunset, an iced coffee on the terrace, a fresh juice after a day on the lake, and a quiet room for the night. The brand should feel like the place: warm, relaxed, a little elegant, proudly Rwandan and at ease with visitors from anywhere.

Warm

We greet everyone like a regular. Murakaza neza, bienvenue, welcome: the first words are always a welcome.

Refined, not fussy

Good glasses, clean design, prices that are easy to read. Nothing loud, nothing cluttered.

Rooted

Lake Kivu, the hills, kitenge on the tables, the K.TOWN sign glowing at night. Local pride in every detail.

Easy for the world

Three languages, clear prices in RWF with a dollar guide, order from the table, pay with MoMo.

Our promise

A great drink, a great view and an easy visit: order from your table, pay when you’re ready, and come back for the next event.

Who we welcome

International travellers and tourists, Rwandan visitors from Kigali and beyond, guests staying in our rooms, and Karongi friends who come for events.

“Where Lake Kivu meets the glass” is a suggested brand line for social media, signs and the future website. The official name stays K.Town Liquor Boutique.

Logo

One glass, one K

The K is a corkscrew opening a glass of wine. Keep it exactly as drawn: always from the original files in assets/img/brand.

Full logo. First choice whenever there is room.

Mark. App icon, small spaces, centre of the QR code.

Wordmark. Wide spaces, next to the mark on photos.

On white and light paper: black and wine.

On wine: white, with rosé wine in the glass.

On black or a dark photo: white and rosé.

Space and size

  • Clear space: keep empty space around the logo at least the height of the “O” in TOWN.
  • Smallest full logo: 25 mm wide in print, 120 px on screen.
  • Smallest mark: 8 mm in print, 32 px on screen.
  • On photos, add a soft dark gradient behind the logo so it stays readable.

Balance

White 60%
Black 30%

  • Wine text on white has a contrast of about 11:1, so it reads well at any size. Rosé is for the logo and decoration, never for small text.
  • For printing, give the printer the HEX and CMYK values and approve a printed proof before a big order: screens and paper show colour differently.
  • Staff screens use amber (preparing) and green (ready) for order status only, always together with a word.
K.Town brand guidelines5
Typography

Two typefaces, two jobs

Both are free Google Fonts (SIL Open Font License) and are already built into the K.Town system.

Jost

The voice of the logo. Round and geometric like the K.TOWN letters. For headings, text, prices, buttons and signs.

Cormorant Garamond

The host’s voice. A classic italic for menu section titles, greetings and short headlines. Never for long text or numbers.

Greeting, Cormorant italicWelcome to K.Town
Menu section, Cormorant italicFresh juice
Item and price, Jost MediumMango juice 5,000
Description, Jost RegularRipe mango, blended smooth.
Table name, Jost Light, spacedTABLE 2
Total, Jost MediumRWF 14,500
  • Write in sentence case. Spaced capitals are kept for table names and the “Liquor Boutique” line, an echo of the wordmark.
  • On menus, show prices without the currency and say “Prices in Rwandan francs (RWF)” once. Totals are written “RWF 14,500”.
  • If Jost is missing in Word or Canva, use Futura or Century Gothic; for Cormorant, use Garamond Italic.
K.Town brand guidelines6
Shape and photography

The pour line

The wave of wine inside the logo is K.Town’s one graphic shape. It finishes a photo, closes a dark panel or divides a page. Once per layout is enough.

White over photos (menu cover, table cards, poster). A thin wine line on paper (printed menu). Wine under dark panels (owner dashboard).

Real K.Town, real light

Do

  • Use our own photos: the lake, the terrace, the sign at night.
  • Natural daylight, one drink as the hero.
  • Show the kitenge, the view or the person enjoying it.
  • Shoot from where the guest sits.

Don’t

  • Use flash or dark, blurry photos.
  • Leave bottles, phones and bills on the table.
  • Use stock photos of other places.
  • Add heavy filters or text on the photo.

QR codes

The K.Town QR code

Wine dots, black corners and the K.Town mark in the centre. The system makes one for every table in Owner dashboard, Tables & QR.

  • Print the code at least 3 cm wide; 4 to 5 cm on table cards (ours are 5 cm).
  • Keep the white margin around it, and always put it on a white panel, never straight on a photo or a dark colour.
  • Keep the web address short (for example ktown-karongi.netlify.app or menu.ktown.rw): shorter addresses make simpler codes that scan faster.
  • Test every printed code with two different phones before it goes on a table.
  • Each table has its own code. Renaming a table keeps the same code, so printed cards keep working.
  • Print again only when the web address changes.
  • Put the code where people sit: in a stand on each table, on the counter, at the entrance.
